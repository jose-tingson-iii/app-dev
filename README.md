## The Hunger Games
**The Hunger Games** is a dystopian sci-fi series about a girl who volunteers to take her sister's place in a deadly televised competition.

### Main Characters
- Katniss Everdeen
- Peeta Mellark
- Gale Hawthorne

> "May the odds be ever in your favor."

## The Hobbit
**The Hobbit** is a fantasy adventure about a hobbit who joins a group of dwarves to reclaim their mountain home from a dragon.

### Main Characters
- Bilbo Baggins
- Gandalf
- Thorin Oakenshield

## The Notebook
**The Notebook** is a romantic drama about a young couple whose love story spans many years.

### Main Characters
- Noah Calhoun
- Allie Hamilton
